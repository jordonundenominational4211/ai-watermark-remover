<h2>🔍 What Is This?</h2>

Have you ever received a text file, a document, or an image that might contain hidden information you can't see? Many AI tools and modern software secretly embed invisible markers into files. These hidden marks—called <strong>watermarks</strong>—can identify who created the file, what AI model made it, or where it came from.



<strong>ai-watermark-remover</strong> is a simple, powerful tool that helps you <em>reveal</em> and <em>strip</em> these hidden marks from text-and files you own. It scans for:

- 🕵️ <strong>Invisible Unicode characters</strong> – characters that are there but you can't see them with your eyes
- 📸 <strong>C2PA</strong> – a modern standard for proving the origin of digital content
- 🏷️ <strong>EXIF/XMP metadata</strong> – hidden data stored inside images and documents (like camera settings, GPS location, author names, etc).



If you want to clean your own files before sharing them, remove tracking marks, or simply know what's hidden inside, this tool gives you control.
